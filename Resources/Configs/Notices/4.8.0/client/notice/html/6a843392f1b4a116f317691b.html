FLEETING MEMORIES COATING RESEARCH RERUN


Four featured coatings have returned in the time-limited special coating research event. Don't sleep on the rare chance!


Fleeting Memories Coating Research Rerun

Duration: 2025/8/13 08:00 - 2025/9/19 01:59 (UTC)


SFX Coatings:

Tainted Moonrise - Luna: Oblivion Exclusive SFX Coating

Grandiose Glamor - Vera: Garnet Exclusive SFX Coating

Neon Dynamite☆ - Karenina: Scire Exclusive SFX Coating

Nightfall Finale - Ishmael: Parhelion Exclusive SFX Coating


Choose your preferred coating to research in this coating research rerun event. You can switch between the coating research pools at any moment during the event. Research progress is tracked separately for each coating.


Research Token

- Each research requires consuming R&D Tokens, which can be exchanged with either Black Cards or Rainbow Cards. The Black Card-to-R&D Token exchange ratio is 1:1, whereas the Rainbow Card-to-R&D Token exchange ratio changes along with the number of researches done.

- The number of tokens needed for each research is listed below: 50/100/250/400/600/800/1200/1700/2200/2700.

- Unused tokens will be converted to Black Cards (1:1) after the event.


Rewards

- The selected SFX coating is guaranteed within 10 researches.

- Research 8 times to obtain the bonus portrait of the coating.

- All rewards are limited in number and can only be obtained once.


Research Rates

- You have a chance to obtain the selected coating starting from the 5th research.

- Check the event notes on the event page for detailed drop rates of the rewards.
