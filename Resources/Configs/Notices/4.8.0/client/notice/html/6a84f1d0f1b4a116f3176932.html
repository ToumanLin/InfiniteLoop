Available Coatings: [Tainted Moonrise], [Grandiose Glamor], [Neon Dynamite ☆], [Nightfall Finale] (for [Luna: Oblivion], [Vera: Garnet], [Karenina: Scire], and [Ishmael: Parhelion] respectively)

Event Details:

- Choose your preferred coating to research in this coating research rerun event. You can switch between the coating research pools at any moment during the event. Research progress is tracked separately for each coating.

- Story rewards for each coating in the Coating Research event can only be claimed once. This limit remains applicable in possible future reruns.

—— Event Rules ——

◆ Reward Type

- All rewards are limited, and cannot be obtained again after drawing.

◆ Reward Details

- Starting from the 5th research, you have a chance to obtain your selected coating.

- Upon reaching 8 research attempts, you will obtain the matching [Coating Portrait] as an extra bonus.

- Research 10 times to guarantee the selected Construct Coating.

◆ Research Costs

- Each Research consumes [Research Items], with the cost increasing each time.

- Cost per Research: 50 / 100 / 250 / 400 / 600 / 800 / 1200 / 1700 / 2200 / 2700

◆ Token Details

- If you don't have enough [Research Items], you can exchange Black Cards or Rainbow Cards for more.

- Black Cards: Converted at a 1:1 ratio. Rainbow Cards: Available at a discounted exchange rate.

- Any remaining [Research Item] will be converted into Black Cards at a 1:1 ratio after the event ends.

VII. New Content & Optimizations

—— New Features & Optimizations ——

1. Added new Full-Realm Support effect "Rending Edge" to [War Zone].

2. Added new Affixes "Tidal Return II" and "Brutal Crash III" to Intensive Battle in [Phantom Pain Cage].

3. Added a complete collection reward for the [Rippling Twilight] set in [Wardrobe]: Nameplate [Unfading Cerulean].

4. Increased the progress cap for rotating zones in [Clash Reflection] to 15, with corresponding new stages, Affixes, challenge missions, and rewards.

5. Improved the visuals of the research animation when obtaining an S-Rank Omniframe.

6. Improved the pop-up trigger for the [Fleeting Memories] Coating Research promo banner.

7. Improved the visuals of the music player's main screen and added playback controls, search, playlists, Immersive Mode, and other features.

8. Fixed an issue where the character models of certain hovering characters would jitter while moving in [Babylonia].

9. Fixed an issue where [Helentine: Lacrimosa] could use the [Generic - Final Alternate Coating] in [Journal of Promise: The Knower's Dilemma].

10. Adjusted the discounted purchase limits for [6★ Memory Resonance Material Pick] in the [War Shop]. Following the [Steering By Light] version update, the first purchase will be free, while the purchase limits at 80% off and 70% off will each be increased to 2.

* Following the [Steering By Light] version update, the discounted purchase limits for [6★ Memory Resonance Material Pick] in the [War Shop] will reset immediately.


—— Character Optimizations ——

1. Removed damage variance from the Physical DMG calculation. Physical DMG will no longer vary, including both CRIT and non-CRIT DMG.

2. Improved the Super Armor granted to [Rosetta: Arete] during Nessos Mimic. She can no longer be thrown by Boss's throw attacks in Nessos Mimic.

3. Fixed an issue where [Kamui: Aeternion] could fail to parry when challenging Bosses in some cases.

4. Fixed an issue where [Nanami: Startrail] could incorrectly inflict "Meltdown" in combat when her Leap Skill "Thunder Synchrony" was only at Level 8.

5. Fixed an issue where [Bianca: Crepuscule]'s legs would slightly jitter while she was idle in combat.

6. Fixed an issue where, in some cases, after switching [Bianca: Crepuscule] out while holding Basic Attack, the first press and hold of Basic Attack would not respond properly when she switched back in.

7. Fixed an issue where defeating the Boss "Lilith" before her phase transition in [Norman Revival Plan] prevented the stage from being completed properly.

8. Fixed an issue in Main Story Stage 26-25 where the Dodge button was not displayed properly, preventing stage progression.

9. Fixed an issue where [Ishmael: Parhelion]'s Class icon was displayed incorrectly when the team included a Physical Tank or Breaker and a Physical Amplifier or Support. This was a display issue only and did not affect actual combat performance.
