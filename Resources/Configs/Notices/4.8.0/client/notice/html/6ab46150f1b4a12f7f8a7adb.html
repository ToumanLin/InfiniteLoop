2) "Crucible Event Construct" & "Fate Crucible Event Construct" Research Instructions

- "Crucible" Construct Research Rules

• Guarantee Rules: The guarantee rules for "Crucible Event" Research are identical to those of the "Themed Construct" Research. You can perform research using [Event Construct R&D Tickets].

• Guarantee Gauge Inheritance Rules: The guarantee gauge is inherited between pools of the same type. For example, after a "Crucible Event Construct Research" has ended, its guarantee progress will be inherited by the next "Crucible Event Construct Research".


- "Crucible" Construct Research Reward Details

• Obtain phase rewards by performing "Crucible Event" Research.

• Phase Reward Rules: For every 20 researches performed, an additional phase reward can be obtained, up to 45 times. Rewards include 6★ Weapon [Unforsaken Beacon], the exclusive CUB [Kelpie], Event Construct R&D Tickets, and more.

• During each period, the "Crucible Event Construct" and "Fate Crucible Event Construct" Research Pools share the same pull count progress.

• When each period of "Crucible Event" Research ends, the phase reward progress will be reset. When the next "Crucible Event" Research begins, the pull count will start accumulating anew. Resetting phase reward progress does not affect the guarantee counter.

• At the end of each "Crucible Event" Research period, unclaimed rewards will be sent via in-game mail. Please claim them promptly.

—— Research Rules ——

The Research rules for "Kurumi Tokisaki Target" and "Fate Kurumi Tokisaki Target" are as follows:

• Research Cost

Cost per Research: [Date A Live Character R&D Ticket] x175. Black Cards exchange for [Date A Live Character R&D Tickets] at a 1:1 ratio.

• Additional Rules

In the "Kurumi Tokisaki Target" and "Fate Kurumi Tokisaki Target" Research Pools, the first two times you obtain Kurumi Tokisaki in each Research Pool, you can claim a bonus copy directly on the Research screen.

*Example: If you obtain Kurumi Tokisaki 1 time in total across the "Kurumi Tokisaki Target" and "Fate Kurumi Tokisaki Target" Research Pools, you will receive 2 copies in total. If you obtain Kurumi Tokisaki 2 times, you will obtain 4 copies in total. If you pull Kurumi Tokisaki 3 times, you will obtain 5 copies in total.

• Inheritance Rules

The "Kurumi Tokisaki Target" and "Fate Kurumi Tokisaki Target" Research Pools are independent pools. Guarantee counters are NOT shared or carried over between them. Please plan your Research accordingly.

VII. New Content & Optimizations

—— New Features & Optimizations ——

1. Added the affixes "Oblivion's Bloom II" and "Steel of Clarity II" to Intensive Battle in [Phantom Pain Cage].

2. Added Base 5 to [Dormitory]. Base 5 contains 6 spaces for creating Dormitories.

3. Increased the storage limit of [Memory Storage] from 1,000 to 1,500.

4. Added [Extinct Star Zone] to [Clash Reflection] and moved [Nightmare Zone: Breach] to Spill Point.

5. Added multiple character and lineup upgrade plans tailored to different needs to [Recommended Equipment].

6. Added "Quick Upgrade" to [Members] - [Train], greatly streamlining character upgrades.

7. After setting an upgrade target under [Members] - [Recommended Equipment], you can now use Quick Upgrade for weapons and CUBs, as well as Quick Equip and Quick Upgrade for Memories.

8. Added the new Class [Overlinker]. After an Overlinker joins the team, all switched-in members gain additional combat buffs. When certain conditions are met, all members enter a special state and gain additional buffs upon triggering Matrix.

9. Added 6★ Weapon Resonance Material x1 and Transition Device x2 to the milestone rewards in [Thunderbolt Manual]. Commandants who claimed the corresponding milestone rewards before the [Anchored in Faith] update will receive the newly added rewards via mail.

- "Transition Device" Details:

• After using a Transition Device on the Quick Upgrade screen, the corresponding character's Level, Promote rank, and skills will be raised to their maximum. This does not apply to Evolve Skills, Leap Skills, or Uniframe Skills.

• If the corresponding character has already been partially trained before using a Transition Device, any training materials already consumed will not be returned.

• The Transition Device works for all characters and can only be used in the Quick Upgrade screen.


10. Added Squad EXP x60 to every mission under [Beginner] - [Novice], for a total of Squad EXP x3,420. Commandants who claimed the corresponding mission rewards before the [Anchored in Faith] update will receive the newly added rewards via mail.

11. Added a display for the remaining duration of the Monthly Pass. The "Advanced Character Serum Monthly Pass Bundle" and "Weapon Resonance Monthly Pass Bundle" will now display their remaining durations.

12. The former "Serum Monthly Pass" and "Weapon R&D Monthly Pass" will be removed after the [Anchored in Faith] update.

- Notes:

• For Commandants who purchased either Pack before the [Anchored in Faith] update, its remaining duration will not be affected by the update.

• After the [Anchored in Faith] update, Commandants who previously purchased either Pack can still purchase the new "Advanced Character Serum Monthly Pass Bundle" and "Weapon Resonance Monthly Pass Bundle". The remaining duration and daily rewards of each Pack will be tracked separately.


13. Adjusted the rewards from the [Monthly Pass]. Its daily rewards now include Serum Bundle β (M) x1. The change will take effect automatically after the [Anchored in Faith] update and will also apply to active Monthly Passes.

14. Adjusted the unlock requirements for Difficulty 5 of [Labyrinth Breakout] in [Shrouded Requiem].

15. Adjusted the amount of Authority Level EXP awarded for completing challenges in [Simulated Battlefield]. Each completed challenge now grants 75 Authority Level EXP.

16. Adjusted the voice-over playback rules for Main Story Stage 0-3. If the system voice-over language is set to Putonghua, the Putonghua voice-over will play. If it is set to Japanese or English, the Japanese voice-over will play.


—— Character Optimizations ——

1. Fixed an issue where the skill camera angle was too high when [Lucia: Crimson Weave] unleashed Shadow Strike during Leaping Thrust.

2. Fixed an issue where [Jetavie: Daybreak] could not cast the skill after pressing and holding a Red Orb until her Absurdum Points were fully charged in some cases.

3. Fixed an issue where the interface buttons might not return to normal after Boss [Ballard]'s grab attack ended if hitstop was triggered during the attack.

4. Fixed an issue where the available QTE uses of certain Amplifier characters did not refresh correctly in some cases when switching members reset the QTE cooldown through their SSS Rank Up Passive.
